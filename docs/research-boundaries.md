# Research Boundaries

PI-engine is designed to test models and structural hypotheses without elevating speculative interpretations into assumptions.

## Evidence lanes

Every cross-domain claim should remain in one of three lanes:

1. **Established mechanism** — experimentally supported, formally defined, or directly observed.
2. **Structural analogy** — a relationship pattern appears useful across domains without asserting identical substrate or cause.
3. **Testable hypothesis** — the analogy predicts a measurable outcome not yet established.

Movement between lanes requires evidence.

## v0.1 epistemic constraints

- Preserve raw provenance and prediction cutoffs.
- Preserve competing models and contradictory evidence.
- Allow `UNIDENTIFIABLE FROM AVAILABLE EVIDENCE`.
- Do not infer causality from correlation alone.
- Do not treat model-space proximity as physical proximity.
- Do not treat information-state contraction as causal state-space contraction.
- Do not force residuals into the dominant theory.
- Do not derive confidence from narrative coherence alone.

## Higher-order hypotheses deliberately withheld from core inference

The following may be studied later as candidate patterns but are not assumptions of v0.1:

- Space / Time / Geometry / Energy as primary global invariants.
- Relationship / Information / Phase / Proximity as universal coupling variables.
- 4+3+1 or 7+1 architectures.
- The Unwritten Seat / glue / web / integrator as a universal +1.
- Nested egg / octave recursion across scales.
- Singularity-like closure or critical-state transitions.
- Literal multiverse or multiple-timeline ontology.
- Human consciousness or intention as a cause of quantum measurement collapse.
- Mythological/archetypal narratives as direct technical descriptions.

These ideas may be used to generate experiments, annotations, or falsifiers. They must not determine outputs by construction.

## Historical and symbolic cases

When analyzing narratives:
- separate source-attested observations from inference and assumption,
- account for transmission, translation, editing, and cultural encoding,
- avoid universal one-symbol/one-meaning decoding,
- allow composite explanations,
- report when multiple hypotheses are observationally indistinguishable.

## Prediction vs intervention

v0.1 predicts trajectories from observed/current state. A later counterfactual layer may ask how trajectory distributions change under hypothetical inputs. Real-world execution or autonomous control is a separate capability and authority domain and is not part of this engine's v0.1 purpose.
